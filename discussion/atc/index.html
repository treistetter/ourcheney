A discussion point

ATC indoor track

Points for neighborhood discussion

Our Cheney created these discussion points to prompt a conversation about potential options. They are not endorsed by any organization and were not developed with Atlanta Track Club, Atlanta Public Schools, or the City of Atlanta. The only purpose is a discussion point to gather community feedback about potential paths forward.

The images are generated from Blender and use U.S. Geological Survey terrain data, USDA aerial photographs, and community-maintained OpenStreetMap streets. The terrain and buildings are accurate enough in dimensions and elevation to show that the concepts are feasible.

The 3,250-seat arena would host 30–40 events during the December–February indoor track season. Off-season events and race number pickups would also likely bring large numbers of people to the track. The building would include offices for 40–50 Atlanta Track Club (ATC) employees, a health clinic, and retail space.

Community access is planned for 30 hours a week. The facility would also include a STEM center whose use is unspecified, Atlanta Public Schools (APS) student use that has not been defined, and commitments to job training programs.
