A discussion point

Greenspace

Points for neighborhood discussion

Our Cheney created these discussion points to prompt a conversation about potential options. They are not endorsed by any organization and were not developed with Atlanta Track Club, Atlanta Public Schools, or the City of Atlanta. The only purpose is a discussion point to gather community feedback about potential paths forward.

The images are generated from Blender and use U.S. Geological Survey terrain data, USDA aerial photographs, and community-maintained OpenStreetMap streets. The models use real-world dimensions and elevations to illustrate how the proposed buildings and uses could fit on the site.

Keeping the site as greenspace faces headwinds, but with neighborhood support, it is likely a feasible path forward. PathFC would continue using its current space, with the remaining space beside the soccer fields fenced off for a dog park. Otherwise, the site would stay as is while the neighborhood works to increase access and improve the space.
