Where to begin

Tell us what maps cannot.

A memory can be a few sentences or a longer story. Positive experiences, concerns, old photographs, and changing patterns of access all belong here.

You might write about…

  • How have you or your family used Cheney Park?
  • How have past investments affected you or your family?
  • Have locked gates, fences, school-hour restrictions, or scheduling rules affected you?
  • Have you contacted APS about access or maintenance?
  • What improvements would make the park more useful and welcoming?
  • What should decision-makers understand before making a long-term agreement?
