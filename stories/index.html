Tell us how lack of local control impacts you

Your Voice Matters

Public decisions are often made using site plans, financial models, and institutional presentations. Those documents rarely show how a place is actually used.

We are collecting stories, photographs, and memories from people who have played, practiced, walked, gathered, or spent time at Cheney Park. We also want to hear about times when fences, locked gates, school-day restrictions, scheduling rules, or other decisions by Atlanta Public Schools made it difficult to use land and facilities that serve the neighborhood.

These experiences can help show what access to this land has meant in practice—and what Summerhill risks losing if future agreements are made without a permanent local voice.
