Community Referendum

Our Cheney is coordinating a community referendum before the Atlanta Track Club project goes to the APS Board. The idea is to provide an accurate picture of local community sentiment for the APS board to act on. We’re looking for volunteers to help share and collect ballots across the neighborhood.

If you are interested in volunteering, fill out the form. The role will be:

  1. Distribute ballots
  2. Provide a place for them to be returned
  3. Canvass until 100% response rate

If the form does not load, open it directly.
