Summerhill's Parks Controlled By Summerhill's Residents

One Goal Local Control

Every generation of Summerhill has been asked to make room for someone else’s vision. This time it will be different. We will negotiate as a community and achieve the best deal for Summerhill together.

The danger is community participation being manufactured into the appearance of community consent. The solution is a recognized community group formally representing the neighborhood.

It starts with organizing and joining our voices together. It ends with delivering this exciting project in partnership with Atlanta Track Club and Atlanta Public Schools.

An Agreement That Works For Summerhill

What We Can Achieve

Local Control

Modeled after Grant Park and Piedmont Park, transfer Phoenix Park II, Phoenix Park III, and the Cheney park from Atlanta Public Schools to a legal conservacy, with permanent stewardship by local residents.

Learn More

Partnership

Share part of the economic value created by the Atlanta Track Club facility with the neighborhood that hosts it, creating a lasting source of funding for park development, recreation, and community programs.

Learn More

Manage Impact

Manage traffic, parking, construction, public access, and long-term maintenance in a way that protects residents today and Summerhill’s future. Create a legally enforceable agreement to ensure the neighborhood is protected.

A valuable opportunity

Equal Partnership

The Atlanta Track Club is a sophisticated institution looking to establish their headquarters. They have financial resources capable of making a serious, long-term commitment to its host neighborhood.

This amazing facility will create new value through events, sponsorships, rentals, concessions, and related activity. Summerhill should share in that success through sustained investment in its parks and community.

See what the public contribution is worth.

We Want This

Yes To The Opportunity

We want the facility here. We want it to thrive. And we want Summerhill residents, APS students, and neighborhood parks to share in its success. Local control gives us the foundation to work with Atlanta Track Club as a true partner rather than simply absorbing the project’s impact.

History teaches us

Summerhill has heard promises before.

Previous investments didn't improve the neighborhood but left it with displacement, broken promises, and parking lots. Decades later we are still trying to rebuild from that "help".

That history does not mean saying no to a new project. It means doing it differently this time. Resident-led, transparent, enforceable, and built to leave Summerhill stronger. Explore our history.

Locked out of our park

Access

Summerhill residents have spent years looking through fences at public fields and green space they cannot reliably use. Our children practice elsewhere while facilities in the center of their own neighborhood remain locked, restricted, or difficult to reserve through APS.
