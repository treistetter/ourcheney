Understand the public contribution

Our Value

This project is not only about a new building. It involves valuable public land, decades of control, and several potential sources of revenue. Before any agreement is finalized, Summerhill should understand the value being contributed and negotiate for a fair return.

Cheney's Value
$30M

A large lot in the middle of a desirable neighborhood

The land

$31.5 million comparable sale

In January 2026, Kaiser Permanente purchased seven acres of former stadium parking lots in Summerhill for $31.5 million, or about $4.5 million per acre.

Atlanta Track Club’s scale

A Major Organizaton

Atlanta Track Club reported approximately $14.2 million in 2024 revenue. Its federal filing reports about $400,000 in total CEO compensation

The Club is now working to raise roughly $100 million for the proposed 175,000-square-foot facility through philanthropy, corporate partners, and tax credits.

These figures are not to criticize. They establish the scale of the organization and the proposed transaction. Summerhill would be negotiating with a capable institution pursuing a major capital project—not a small volunteer track club.

The negotiating position

Summerhill should negotiate as an equal.

Atlanta Track Club is a sophisticated counterparty with millions in annual revenue and a roughly $100 million capital goal. Summerhill needs an equally serious structure representing the neighborhood’s interests.

Local control would give residents the organization, information, and negotiating strength needed to protect the value of our land and secure a fair, lasting share of the opportunity created here.
