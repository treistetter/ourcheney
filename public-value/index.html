$31.5 million comparable sale

In January 2026, Kaiser Permanente purchased seven acres of former stadium parking lots in Summerhill for $31.5 million, or about $4.5 million per acre.

Atlanta Track Club’s scale

Organization Scale

Atlanta Track Club reported approximately $14.2 million in 2024 revenue. Its federal filing reports about $400,000 in total CEO compensation.

The Club is working to raise roughly $100 million for the proposed 175,000-square-foot facility through philanthropy, corporate partners, and tax credits.

These figures provide context for the size of the organization and its proposed capital project.

The transaction in context

Our Land for Private HQ

This is not an empty field being given to a charity for public use. It is likely the single most valuable lot in Summerhill being given to a revenue driven organization. The public value won't be returned to the residents of Summerhill, Atlanta, or Georgia. It will be used to drive more revenue for the Atlanta Track Club while residents pay for access to their own public land.
