From participation to accountability

Plan

Summerhill needs a clear process that protects the meaning of participation, establishes a unified community voice, and turns public promises into an agreement the neighborhood can enforce.

Five steps

A path to a fair agreement.

  1. 01

    Protect the meaning of consent

    Stop Participation

    Participation will be presented as consent while the Atlanta Track Club and Atlanta Public Schools do what they want. Don't give them that power. Instead, provide your feedback through Our Cheney and help us establish a fair and transparent approval process.

  2. 02

    Bargain with one community voice

    Secure Our Voice

    Establish Our Cheney as the community’s recognized bargaining partner. We will speak with one voice and secure our part in the planning process. Plans and promises will be given in writing instead of verbal promises with no accountability.

  3. 03

    Make participation transparent

    Add Our Input

    Create a transparent process with public information, open meetings, written proposals, clear response periods, and a record of how resident input changes the plan. Everyone should be able to see what was proposed, what the community said, and what happened next.

  4. 04

    Require community approval

    Vote

    We are parents, runners, citizens, and have long experience with empty promises. The community is best positioned to ensure that the agreement works for every child, every runner, and every community member. Community approval is the path towards the best usage of this public land.

  5. 05

    Make the deal deliver

    Enforce

    Long after the splashy headlines are written and the photo ops with bigwigs happen we will still be here. We will still be parents, runners, and residents invested in our community. We will ensure that promises made today will be kept decades from now.

Start with one voice

Organize before we negotiate.

The first step is showing that Summerhill expects a fair, transparent, and enforceable process.
