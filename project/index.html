The proposed development

The Project

Atlanta Track Club has proposed an indoor competition venue and parking lot on public-school land at Cheney.

The proposal at a glance

A major venue and a long-term public-land decision.

Atlanta Track Club proposes replacing the athletic field north of Cheney Stadium with a 3,250-person indoor track venue and converting the neighboring soccer fields into parking. The facility is intended to host events ranging from local meets to nationally broadcast championships.
