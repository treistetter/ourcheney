Public land, protected

A Better Ownership Model

Public land should remain public, protected, and accountable to the people who use it. A stronger ownership model preserves the park permanently and gives Summerhill residents a meaningful role in its future.

A clear structure

Public ownership with local stewardship.

This framework keeps the land in public ownership, establishes lasting protection, and gives a resident-centered nonprofit clear stewardship responsibility. The governing details will be established through public agreements and legal review.

Atlanta Public Schools Current public owner
Protected public parkland Long-term legal protections
Resident-centered nonprofit conservancy Steward—not private owner

Protection for future generations

Established Model

A conservancy is a well trod path forward for a community park. It's a clear ownership model that benefits the community and will endure for generations.

A locally run, not for profit organization will coordinate programs, hold events, support maintenance, and advocate for the long-term health of the park. All focused on what's best for residents.

A non-profit focused on the park

Local Stewardship

Financial security for generations

Funding that lasts

The conservancy will raise funds from public and private sources, receive fees for events, and charge usage fees. All in an endowment to ensure the future of the park.

A foundation for partnership

Local stewardship. Permanent protection.

Summerhill should have more than occasional input into decisions about its public land. A resident-centered conservancy gives the neighborhood a lasting role, preserves public ownership, and creates a stronger foundation for partnership with APS, public institutions, and Atlanta Track Club.
